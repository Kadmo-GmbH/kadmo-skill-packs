# Plain English Alternatives

Replace complex words with their plain alternatives. Simpler words are faster to read, harder to misunderstand, and make copy feel more human. When two alternatives are listed, pick the one that fits the sentence rhythm.

## Phrases to Remove Entirely

These phrases add no meaning. Delete them and the sentence will say the same thing:

- "It is important to note that"
- "It should be noted that"
- "As a matter of fact"
- "In order to" (replace with "to")
- "Due to the fact that" (replace with "because")
- "In the event that" (replace with "if")
- "At the present time" (replace with "now")
- "At this point in time" (replace with "now")
- "For the purpose of" (replace with "to" or "for")
- "In terms of" (replace with "for" or rephrase)
- "With regard to" (replace with "about")
- "In light of the fact that" (replace with "because" or "since")
- "It goes without saying" (then don't say it)
- "Needless to say" (then don't say it)
- "As previously mentioned"
- "In today's world"
- "In this day and age"
- "Each and every"
- "First and foremost"
- "Any and all"
- "One of the most"
- "The fact that"
- "When it comes to"
- "At the end of the day"
- "On a daily basis" (replace with "daily")
- "In a timely manner" (replace with "on time" or "quickly")
- "Take into consideration" (replace with "consider")
- "Make a decision" (replace with "decide")
- "Reach out to" (replace with "contact" or "email")
- "In close proximity to" (replace with "near")
- "A large number of" (replace with "many")
- "On the grounds that" (replace with "because")
